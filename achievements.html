Big Walk Achievements

Big Walk ties several achievements to exploration milestones: finishing the Red Tower, wearing a backpack, and reaching the island's highest point.

Known Achievements

AchievementHow to UnlockSource
Big ViewComplete the Red Tower map room and view the full-scale map diorama.Red Tower guide
Big PackEquip a backpack for the first time. Backpacks add an extra item slot.Backpack guide
Big ClimbReach the highest point on the island.ProdigyGamers 100% guide

Scope note: this list is not an official full list. ProdigyGamers published a Big Walk 100% achievement guide and full walkthrough on August 5, 2026; use it as a reference for the complete set.

Tips for Achievement Hunting

  • Finish the Red Tower early to get the map; the map marks completed areas and makes exploration achievements easier.
  • Grab a backpack as soon as you reach the Red Tower area; the Big Pack achievement unlocks simply by wearing it once.
  • Keep a map or compass equipped while exploring high ground if you are aiming for Big Climb.
