A sign that is actually a location

Big Walk 4166 / 1899 Coordinate Puzzle

One of Big Walk's late-game signs shows numbers such as 4166 / 1899. Polygon's solution explains that the numbers are coordinates: the answer is essentially across the map from the sign.

How to Solve It

  1. Read the sign carefully. The pair of numbers on the sign is not a code to enter; it is a map location.
  2. Treat the numbers as coordinates. Match the two numbers to a position on the island's map.
  3. Travel to the coordinate point. The destination is effectively on the other side of the map from where you find the sign.
  4. Interact with what you find there to complete the puzzle and collect the reward.

Review note: Game8's review flags this puzzle as one of the weaker moments because it hints at map coordinates before players may have access to the information needed to interpret them. Having the map from the Red Tower makes the connection much easier.
