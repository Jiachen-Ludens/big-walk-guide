Questions and Answers

QuestionAnswer
When did Big Walk release?August 4, 2026, on Nintendo Switch 2, Steam (PC and Mac), and PlayStation 5.
How much does it cost?$19.99 USD, with a limited-time 25% launch discount. The Canadian Switch 2 store lists CAD 19.49.
Is there a single-player mode?No. Big Walk is a multiplayer game without a single-player mode.
How many players?2 to 12 players, with flexible group size.
Is it crossplay?Yes. Nintendo's page lists crossplay: play online with friends on other platforms.
Is it on PlayStation Plus?Yes, it is available through the PlayStation Plus Essential tier on PS5.
Does it have an ending?Yes. Nintendo describes Big Walk as a hand-crafted cooperative adventure with an ending.
What are the review scores?Game8 reported a 94 Metacritic score on PC on launch day, the highest of 2026 at that time.
Does the game have performance problems?Game8's reviewer saw unstable performance on Medium settings on recommended hardware and noted low default proximity chat volume.
Who made Big Walk?House House (Untitled Goose Game, Push Me Pull You) developed it; Panic (Firewatch, Thank Goodness You're Here!) published it.
